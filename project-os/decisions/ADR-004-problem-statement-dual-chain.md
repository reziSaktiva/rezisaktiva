# Decision ADR-004

### Title

Problem statement: dual framing + visibility -> narrative -> evaluation chain

### Status

Accepted

### Date

2026-08-06

### Decision

1. **Framing:** Dual - the brand owner's problem (Rezi) and the primary audience's problem (founder / product owner) are written side by side.
2. **Current situation:** Accounts exist on LinkedIn, GitHub, CV, Instagram, Threads, and X, but there is no presence that makes Rezi known/stand out; real activity is mostly GitHub because of work; there is no portfolio site as a public source of truth.
3. **Core problem:** The full chain - weak visibility -> narrative is not yet coherent (defaults to "developer") -> evaluation and contact have friction for founders/POs.
4. **Target solution:** One owned digital home that controls the product builder narrative and makes quick evaluation easier; other platforms remain satellites, not replacements.

### Reason

- Dual framing keeps Rezi's needs honest while staying relevant to the primary audience (aligned with brand-first ADR-002).
- The full chain reflects reality: the issue is not just "not enough content", but recognition -> story -> decision.
- Separating "has accounts" from "has a standout presence" prevents the wrong solution (being active everywhere for its own sake).

### Alternatives Considered

- Audience-only or owner-only framing - rejected; Boss Rezi chose dual.
- Focus only on visibility or only on narrative - rejected; Boss Rezi chose the full chain.
- Rely on LinkedIn/GitHub as the main solution without an owned site - rejected; it does not give control over the product builder narrative.
