# Decision ADR-007

### Title

Business model: brand + soft inbound; growth = destination + light magnet

### Status

Accepted

### Date

2026-08-07

### Decision

1. The **value model** stays aligned with ADR-002: brand first; job/client secondary soft; **no direct revenue from the site** in v0.1.
2. **Growth is locked (option B):** destination (one official URL) + **light magnet** (short case/process content on the site).
3. Not MVP: systematic active distribution (option C) or ship-in-public as the main engine (option D).
4. Public pricing / shop / SaaS on the site remains out of scope; engagement pricing details -> `pricing-strategy.md` (may be N/A).

### Reason

- Brand equity + soft inbound keep the message clear and opportunity quality high.
- A light magnet supports the visibility -> narrative chain without forcing a blog/media or social hustle at the start.
- Proportional for a solo builder: time investment stays focused, while craft and distribution can grow gradually.

### Alternatives Considered

- A Destination only - rejected; not enough narrative/share material on the site.
- C Destination + active distribution - deferred; high effort for MVP.
- D Ship-in-public as the main engine - rejected; risk of shifting focus away from founder/PO evaluation.
- B Destination + light magnet - accepted.
