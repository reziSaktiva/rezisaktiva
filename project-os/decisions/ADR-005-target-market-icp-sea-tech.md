# Decision ADR-005

### Title

Target market: equal founder/PO hybrid ICP, SEA focus, digital product/tech domain

### Status

Accepted

### Date

2026-08-06

### Decision

1. **Primary remains founder / product owner** (aligned with ADR-002), expanded into two **equal** archetypes:
   - Early founder / indie builder
   - Product owner in a company / startup
2. **Core geography focus:** SEA (Indonesia + neighbors); outside SEA remains open as secondary.
3. **ICP domain:** digital product / tech (SaaS, app, platform, tools) - not unlimited cross-industry scope.
4. ADR-002 secondary audiences (hiring manager/tech lead, prospective client, peer) remain supported without shifting the primary message.

### Reason

- Equal hybrid avoids a message that is too "indie only" or too "corporate hire only"; both evaluate a product builder.
- SEA aligns with home base + geo-aware bilingual support, without closing the door to global opportunities.
- The digital product domain keeps positioning relevant (product builder + fullstack + AI edge).

### Alternatives Considered

- Only early founder, or only company PO - rejected; Boss Rezi chose equal (option C).
- Indonesia first / global equal / Indonesia only - rejected; SEA focus was chosen.
- Tech-first open to non-tech, cross-industry, or narrow vertical niche - rejected; a clear digital product/tech domain was chosen.
