# Overview

**rezisaktiva** is a personal portfolio website for presenting the work, experience, and professional identity of Rezi Saktiva as a **product builder** with a full-stack foundation and AI as an edge. Value model: brand first, with job opportunities and client/project paths as secondary routes (soft CTA).

Full details for vision, scope, and audience live in the `../product-discovery/` baseline (see `product-vision.md`, `problem-statement.md`, `target-market.md`).

---

# Goals

* Help visitors (founders/product owners; secondarily hiring managers and potential clients) quickly understand Rezi's product-builder positioning in one short visit.
* Present credibility proof (full-stack experience, work, way of working) without fluff through Home, About, Workflow, and the Work index.
* Open a natural contact path (Contact modal) without feeling hard-sell.

Full details: `../product-discovery/01-business/product-vision.md` (ADR-002, ADR-009).

---

# Non-Goals

* Not a visual design portfolio / design agency.
* Not a store/e-commerce/public SaaS as the site's core business.
* Not an aggressive sales funnel (hard pricing, hard sell, heavy lead magnet).
* Not a blog/large media site as the main R1 focus.

Full details: `../product-discovery/01-business/product-vision.md` § Out of Scope.
